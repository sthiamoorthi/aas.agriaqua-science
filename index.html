About the journal

Research for a more sustainable future

AgriAqua Science (AAS Publications) is being developed as a scholarly platform for research and knowledge exchange across agricultural sciences, aquaculture, fisheries and environmental sciences.

The journal aims to provide a clear route for researchers, academicians and postgraduate scholars to share original findings, reviews and applied research relevant to sustainable food systems and natural resource management.

Scope of the Journal

We welcome relevant, methodologically sound research across connected life-science and sustainability disciplines.

🌾

Agricultural Sciences

Agronomy, crop science, soil science, horticulture, plant biology, organic farming, crop protection and plant biotechnology.

🐟

Aquaculture & Fisheries

Fish biology, fish nutrition, aquatic animal health, shrimp culture, biofloc technology, fisheries management and aquatic biotechnology.

🌿

Environment & Sustainability

Environmental science, biodiversity, ecology, conservation, climate change, pollution, integrated farming and natural resource management.
